## Official meaning of the Div Yield field

NSE defines dividend yield as:

Dividend Yield (%) = Gross Dividend / Index Market Capitalisation × 100

Gross dividend includes final, interim and special dividends reported by index constituents over a rolling 12-month period, calculated using ex-dividend dates and adjusted for index factors such as free float and capping.
